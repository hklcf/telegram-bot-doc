# getBusinessAccountStarBalance

> Part of the [Telegram Bot API](../../api.md). Canonical location: [getBusinessAccountStarBalance](../../api.md#getbusinessaccountstarbalance).

Returns the amount of Telegram Stars owned by a managed business account. Requires the *can_view_gifts_and_stars* business bot right. Returns [StarAmount](../types/staramount.md) on success.

|Parameter|Type|Required|Description|
|-----------|
|business_connection_id|String|Yes|Unique identifier of the business connection|
